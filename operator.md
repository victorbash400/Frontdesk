Operator

Operator is an AI customer agent that acts as the bridge between a business and its customers.

Instead of functioning like a chatbot, ticketing system, or CRM assistant, Operator handles customer relationships directly. It talks to customers, understands what they need, checks the business systems for the information required, takes action when it can, and brings in a human only when a decision or intervention is actually necessary.

Operator can communicate with customers through channels such as phone calls and email. Customers can contact Operator when they need help, and Operator can also reach out proactively when the business needs something from them.

Each customer has persistent context. Operator remembers previous conversations, relevant details, preferences, requests, commitments, and what has happened in the relationship before. When a customer returns, Operator continues from that context rather than treating every interaction as a new support request.

Operator connects to the systems the business already uses. Depending on the business, this could include order databases, inventory, scheduling systems, billing, delivery systems, product information, internal tools, support systems, or other operational data.

When a customer asks a question, Operator can inspect those systems before responding. If someone asks where an order is, Operator checks the actual order. If they want to change something, Operator checks whether the change is still possible and updates the relevant system when appropriate. If they need an appointment moved, Operator can inspect availability and arrange it. If they need a document, invoice, confirmation, or other information, Operator can retrieve and send it.

Operator can also handle onboarding. When a new customer joins, it can contact them, collect missing information, explain what happens next, arrange appointments or deliveries, send documents, answer questions, and follow up when something remains unfinished.

The same applies throughout the customer relationship. Operator can follow up on unresolved requests, contact customers when the business needs clarification, notify them when something changes, coordinate deliveries or appointments, send updates, and continue conversations across different channels.

Operator does not force customers into predefined stages, scores, workflows, or categories. It treats each customer as a continuing relationship and works from the context of what is actually happening.

When Operator reaches something it should not decide on its own, it contacts the appropriate person in the business with the relevant context. The human can make the decision, and Operator then continues handling the customer from there.

The goal of Operator is to reduce the amount of staff time spent acting as the connection between customers and internal business systems.

Customers still get a responsive, conversational experience. Businesses spend less time answering routine questions, looking up information, coordinating simple changes, following up, onboarding customers, and relaying information between people and systems.

In simple terms:

Operator gives every customer an always-available representative that can talk to them, understand them, work inside the business on their behalf, and involve staff only when they are actually needed.
